How to follow Optimal Momentum

Step 1 — Once a month

On the 1st business day of each month, check the signal on this site or wait for the email notification. The rebalance takes ~5 minutes.

Step 2 — Check the regime

The strategy has two modes. If the S&P 500 is above its 250-day moving average: hold momentum stocks. If it is below: sell everything and buy GLD (SPDR Gold Shares ETF, ticker: GLD).

Step 3 — Execute

In momentum mode: sell previous holdings and buy the 7 stocks listed in equal weight (~14.3% each). In gold mode: hold GLD until the regime flips back.

Key details

Universe: US large-cap equities, market cap >$10B, positive operating income. Composite momentum = 50% 6-month + 50% 12-month return. No intra-month changes — set and forget until next 1st.

How to follow the GDX Weekly Signal

Step 1 — Check every Friday evening

After NYSE close (~4pm ET), the signal is computed and sent automatically via Telegram. It shows BUY or CASH for the following week.

Step 2 — The rule

If GDX (VanEck Gold Miners ETF) returned more than +2% this week, the signal is BUY. Otherwise, stay in cash. One condition, no interpretation needed.

Step 3 — Execute Monday open

On BUY weeks: buy equal weight Gold + Silver at Monday open. Sell at Friday close. Instruments: GLD + SLV (ETFs, any broker) or GC + SI futures.

Key details

Strategy is invested ~35% of the time (~18 weeks/year). Signal based on weekly closing prices. Backtest 2006–2026, 20 years, 18 of 20 years positive. Not investment advice.

How to follow Coiled Spring

Step 1 — Once a month

On the 1st business day of each month, check the signal on this site. The rebalance takes ~10 minutes.

Step 2 — Check the regime

If the S&P 500 is above its 225-day moving average: hold the 10 stocks. If it is below: sell everything and stay in cash until the regime flips back.

Step 3 — Execute

Buy the 10 stocks listed in equal weight (10% each). Sell the previous month's holdings that are no longer in the top 10.

Key details

Universe: US equities, market cap ≥$1B, positive operating income, 6-month return between −15% and +15% (lateral price action). Signal = (ROIC + NM growth + FCF growth + FCF level) / 4, all cross-sectional percentile ranks.

How to follow UK Optimal Momentum

Step 1 — Once a month

On the 1st business day of each month, check the signal on this site. Takes ~5 minutes to execute.

Step 2 — Check the regime

If the FTSE 100 is above its 200-day moving average: hold the 7 UK stocks. If it is below: sell everything and stay in cash until the regime flips back. The strategy is in cash ~33% of the time.

Step 3 — Execute

Buy the 7 stocks listed in equal weight (~14.3% each) on your UK broker (e.g. Interactive Brokers, Hargreaves Lansdown). Sell the previous month's holdings no longer in the top 7.

Key details

Universe: UK-listed equities (LSE, GBX-denominated), market cap >£250M, positive operating income. Signal = pure 6-month return ranking. No intra-month changes — set and forget until next 1st.

How to follow the Bitcoin Trend Signal

Step 1 — Receive the daily signal

The signal updates every day after market close and is sent automatically to the Telegram channel — no need to check the site manually. It shows BUY or OUT clearly.

Step 2 — Two positions only

The strategy is binary: either fully invested in Bitcoin (spot, on your exchange of choice) or fully in cash. No partial positions, no leverage.

Step 3 — Act on signal changes

You only need to act when the signal changes. If it was BUY yesterday and is BUY today — do nothing. When it flips to OUT: sell. When it flips to BUY: buy. On average the signal changes ~22 times per year.

Key details

The strategy is invested ~34% of the time. Cash earns 0% in the backtest — any yield on idle cash is upside. Signal is based on daily closing prices.
