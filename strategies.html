How to follow USA Momentum

Step 1 — Once a month

On the 1st business day of each month, check the signal on this site or wait for the email notification. The rebalance takes ~5 minutes.

Step 2 — Check the regime

The strategy has two modes. If the S&P 500 is above its 250-day moving average: hold momentum stocks. If it is below: sell everything and move to cash.

Step 3 — Execute

In momentum mode: sell previous holdings and buy the 7 stocks listed in equal weight (~14.3% each). In cash mode: hold cash until the regime flips back.

How to follow the GDX Weekly Signal

Step 1 — Check every Friday evening

After NYSE close (~4pm ET), the signal is computed and sent automatically via Telegram. It shows BUY or CASH for the following week.

Step 2 — The rule

If GDX (VanEck Gold Miners ETF) returned more than +2% this week, the signal is BUY. Otherwise, stay in cash. One condition, no interpretation needed.

Step 3 — Execute Monday open

On BUY weeks: buy equal weight Gold + Silver at Monday open. Sell at Friday close. Instruments: GLD + SLV (ETFs, any broker) or GC + SI futures.

Key details

Strategy is invested ~35% of the time (~18 weeks/year). Signal based on weekly closing prices. Backtest 2006–2026, 20 years, 18 of 20 years positive. Not investment advice.

How to follow the Bitcoin Trend Signal

Step 1 — Receive the daily signal

The signal updates every day after market close and is sent automatically to the Telegram channel — no need to check the site manually. It shows BUY or OUT clearly.

Step 2 — Two positions only

The strategy is binary: either fully invested in Bitcoin (spot, on your exchange of choice) or fully in cash. No partial positions, no leverage.

Step 3 — Act on signal changes

You only need to act when the signal changes. If it was BUY yesterday and is BUY today — do nothing. When it flips to OUT: sell. When it flips to BUY: buy. On average the signal changes ~22 times per year.

Key details

The strategy is invested ~34% of the time. Cash earns 0% in the backtest — any yield on idle cash is upside. Signal is based on daily closing prices.
