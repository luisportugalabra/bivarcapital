When you rebalance matters more than which strategy you use.

We ran the same value strategy 12 times — once starting each month — across 21 years (2005–2026). Same stocks, same rules. January rebalance: +12.7% CAGR. July rebalance: +4.9%. A 7.8% annual gap from timing alone. Tax-loss selling in December creates structural underpricing of value stocks that reverses in January. The effect is persistent, well-documented, and unlikely to be arbitraged away.
