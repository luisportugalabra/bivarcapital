IV. Execution Delay Impact

Signal computed at close of day T. How much alpha is lost if you execute at close of T+1 or T+2?

1-day delay costs ~20pp CAGR for the full strategy (86% → 64%) and ~3pp for pure MA (76% → 73%). Pure MA is more robust to delay because it trades less frequently (6/yr vs 25/yr). In practice, you execute ~8 hours after the signal, not 24h, so the real impact is somewhere in between.

V. Verdict

Recommended: RSI14 > 54 + Price > MA155 + Vol < 100% + 2% exit buffer + 3-day cooldown.

Backtest Sharpe 2.35, realistic (delay 1d) Sharpe 1.71. MaxDD −28% to −35%. ~20 trades per year. Telegram alert at 00:10 UTC daily.

Alternative (simpler): Pure MA155. Sharpe 1.37 with delay. Only 6 trades per year. But MaxDD −64% — double the drawdown. Choose based on your risk tolerance.
