VI. Strategy Rules

Optimal Momentum (65% of portfolio)

Signal: last trading day of month, execute: first trading day of next month:

  1. S&P 500 below MA250? → Buy GLD
  2. Momentum = 50% × 6-month + 50% × 12-month return
  3. Filter: mcap >$10B, EBIT > 0, US common stock
  4. Top 7 by momentum (no sector cap)
  5. Equal weight (~14.3% each)

Backtest 2005–2026: CAGR +29.1%, Sharpe 1.03, MaxDD −29.1%

BTC Systematic (35% of portfolio)

Check daily at close, execute next day:

  1. RSI(14) > 54
  2. Price > 155-day Moving Average
  3. 20-day Annualised Volatility < 100%
  4. All true → BUY BTC
  5. Any fails → SELL, cash

Backtest 2015–2026: CAGR +64.1% (1d delay), Sharpe 1.71, MaxDD −34.8%

X. Risks & Caveats

  • 11 years of combined data. BTC only exists since 2015. The combined backtest is short.
  • BTC may change character. If BTC becomes less trend-following, the systematic strategy degrades.
  • Transaction costs: ~84 stock trades/year + ~20 BTC trades/year. Estimate −1 to −2% CAGR.
  • Overfitting: Both strategies were optimised. Expect 20–30% Sharpe degradation live.
  • Correlation may increase: In a systemic crisis, correlations spike. The −17% MaxDD could be worse.
  • Realistic expectation: CAGR +35–45%, Sharpe 1.0–1.3 after costs and degradation.
