3. Market Regime Filter

The strategy uses a daily synthetic market index constructed from Euronext Amsterdam stocks (cap-weighted by P40 mcap threshold). The index is compared to its 100-day moving average (MA100).

  • Momentum regime: Index ≥ MA100 → Buy top 10 momentum stocks
  • Defensive regime: Index < MA100 → Go to cash (0% equity exposure)

The regime is evaluated monthly at end-of-month. This filter dramatically reduced drawdowns during the 2000–2002 dotcom crash, the 2008 GFC, and the 2022 bear market—while remaining invested during most of the 2009–2021 bull market.

The 100-day window was selected via grid search over N=10 portfolios across MA periods of None, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300 days. MA100 produced the highest Sharpe ratio of 2.057.

Methodology

At each month-end, eligible stocks (mcap >92M EUR, in top 60% by mcap) are ranked by their 12-month return excluding the most recent month (12-1 momentum). The top 10 are selected. Weights are computed using inverse-volatility (1/σ), normalized to sum to 1. If the synthetic market index is below its 100-day moving average at the signal date, the portfolio goes 100% to cash.
