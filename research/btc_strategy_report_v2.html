X. Caveats

Short history. 11 years. BTC may change as institutional adoption grows.

Overfitting. Parameters optimised on this data. Expect 20–30% Sharpe degradation.

Costs. ~22 trades/yr × 0.2% = ~4%/yr drag. Not in backtest.

Cash drag. In cash 66% of the time at 0% yield.
