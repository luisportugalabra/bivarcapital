Investment Thesis

In late 2025 Coupang suffered the largest data breach in Korean corporate history — roughly 34M accounts — followed in 2026 by a record KRW 624.7B (~$430M) administrative fine. Q2 2026 shows the full cost of the cleanup: a $570M net loss, gross margin down 188bps, Product Commerce EBITDA margin down 390bps to 5.1%, as the company carries excess capacity and elevated marketing spend to win back customers, all while the won moved against the reported numbers.

The bear case is that the breach permanently damaged the franchise. The evidence disagrees: active customers grew 3% YoY through the worst of it, FX-neutral revenue per customer grew 5%, Product Commerce grew 8% FX-neutral, and Developing Offerings (Taiwan, Eats, Play, Farfetch) grew 24% while narrowing losses. Customers did not leave. They shopped slightly less, and Coupang paid up — once — to bring activity back.

You are paying ~0.74× sales for Korea's dominant e-commerce platform — a business the market valued at over 1.5× sales a year ago — because of a fine that has already been recognized and a margin dip management has explicitly guided to reverse by mid-2027.

Bull Points

01 — The Fine Is Recognized, One-Off, and Survivable

Of the $570M Q2 net loss, $410M was administrative fines — adjusted net loss was $160M. The company holds $6.1B in cash against $3.7B of debt. The worst regulatory outcome is now a booked number, not an open-ended threat, and it amounts to ~1.4% of market cap.

02 — The Customer Base Held

24.7M active customers, up 3% YoY, measured after the breach dominated Korean headlines for months. Revenue per customer of $301 was down 2% reported but +5% in constant currency. A franchise suffering permanent reputational damage does not put up those numbers. Rocket Delivery's same-day/dawn delivery network remains the reason: there is no substitute service to churn to.

03 — The Margin Dip Is Spending, Not Structure

Product Commerce EBITDA margin fell from ~9% to 5.1% — driven by reacquisition marketing, goodwill promotions, and deliberately retained excess fulfillment capacity. These are choices, not costs imposed by competition. Management has guided to full margin recovery by mid-2027. Every quarter between now and then is a checkpoint the market can mark to.

04 — Developing Offerings Is Quietly Working

+24% FX-neutral growth with losses narrowing ($219M loss, improved YoY). Taiwan is scaling on the Korean playbook, Eats is taking share in food delivery, and Farfetch — bought for scraps in 2024 — stopped bleeding. A segment the market models at zero (or negative) is compounding at 20%+.

05 — The Moat Did Not Get Breached

The breach hit a database, not the physical network: the fulfillment centers within 7 miles of ~70% of Koreans, the last-mile fleet, the 30M+ SKUs on Rocket. That infrastructure took a decade and $10B+ to build. Naver and the traditional retailers still cannot match next-dawn delivery at scale. The asset that justified $50B+ valuations is intact.

06 — Founder-Led, Long-Horizon Ownership

Bom Kim controls the company via dual-class shares and has consistently chosen decade-scale bets (Taiwan, Eats, WOW membership pricing) over quarterly optics. The same governance that annoys traders is what lets the company spend through a crisis instead of managing EPS through it.

Risks & Bear Points

01

The churn tail is not fully visible yet. Reported revenue per customer is still negative YoY and management admits activity has not fully recovered. If Q3/Q4 show reacquisition spend becoming permanent rather than temporary, the margin-recovery thesis breaks.

02

FCF is effectively zero. TTM free cash flow of $105M on a $28.6B market cap. The valuation argument rests entirely on normalized 2027+ earnings power, not on cash being generated today.

03

Regulatory overhang beyond the fine. Korean regulators (KFTC, PIPC) have Coupang's market power in their sights structurally — the breach gave political cover for a harder line on everything from seller fees to WOW bundling.

04

KRW translation. ~85% of revenue is won-denominated. The 6pp gap between reported and FX-neutral growth this quarter cuts both ways; a weaker won makes the dollar numbers look sick even when the business is fine.

05

Korea is a mature market. E-commerce penetration is among the highest on earth. Ex-Taiwan, long-run growth converges to Korean consumption growth — mid single digits. The terminal multiple should respect that.

Verdict

The breach cost Coupang a fine, a bad year of margins, and half its market cap. Only one of those three is permanent — and it's the smallest.

ASYMMETRIC LONG · SIZE FOR A VOLATILE 12-18 MONTHS · THESIS BREAKS IF PC MARGINS DON'T INFLECT BY MID-2027

At 0.74× EV/Sales you are being paid to underwrite a single question: was the damage to customer behavior temporary? Three data points — customers +3%, FX-neutral spend per customer +5%, PC revenue +8% FX-neutral — say yes. The stock near its 52-week low says the market hasn't looked. We'd rather own that disagreement than most things at this price.
