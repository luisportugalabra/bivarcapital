Methodology

  1. For each calendar day N (1 through 30), the signal date is the first trading day on or after day N of each month. If day N falls on a weekend or holiday, the next trading day is used. If day N exceeds the month’s length (e.g. day 30 in February), the last trading day of the month is used.
  2. The execution date (buy/sell) is the next trading day after the signal date.
  3. On the signal date: check S&P 500 vs MA250. If below → buy GLD (gold futures GC=F scaled before Nov 2004). If above → rank stocks by composite momentum (50% × 126-day return + 50% × 252-day return), filter by mcap >$10B and EBIT >0, select top 7, equal weight.
  4. No sector cap. GLD during bear months. US domestic common stock only.
  5. Period: January 1998 – April 2026. Data: Sharadar daily prices + fundamentals + Yahoo Finance (GLD/GC=F).
  6. Sharpe ratio uses risk-free rate of 3% annualised.
Note on robustness: The strategy is profitable on every single day of the month, with CAGR ranging from +20.5% to +26.9% and Sharpe from 0.75 to 0.93. The momentum premium is real and not dependent on a specific signal day. The choice of signal day affects magnitude but not direction.
