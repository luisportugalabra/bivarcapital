Strategy Rules
How it works
01
Risk-Adjusted Momentum Signal
Signal = 21-month return (skip 1m) ÷ 12-month rolling volatility. Ranks stocks by return per unit of risk — filters erratic spikes, favours consistent trends.
02
Universe
All China A-shares (Shanghai + Shenzhen), ~5,400 stocks. Excludes ST-flagged stocks (regulatory distressed flag). No minimum market cap filter.
03
Portfolio Construction
Top 10 stocks by risk-adjusted signal. Equal weight (~10% each). Monthly rebalance at end of month.
04
Regime Filter
CSI300 must be above its 150-day moving average. When below → 100% cash. Invested ~53% of the time. MA150 is optimal vs MA100/120/200 tested.
