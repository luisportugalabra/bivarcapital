Momentum in China A-SharesDifferent rules. Longer horizons.

Momentum works in China — but not the way it works in the US. Short lookbacks destroy wealth. Long lookbacks of 20–22 months capture the signal. A regime filter is not optional: without it, every configuration collapses in bear markets. We test 5,400 A-shares from 2009 to 2026.

Best configuration: 22-month lookback, skip 1, top 7, MA150 regime filter. +12.4% CAGR, Sharpe 0.35, max drawdown −52.7%. The strategy is invested 49% of the time — it spends roughly half its life in cash, entirely driven by the CSI 300 trend signal. ¥100,000 grows to ¥574,000 over 17 years vs ¥264,000 for the CSI 300.

This is significantly weaker than US momentum. The equivalent US strategy (6-month lookback, no skip) delivers Sharpe 0.83 and CAGR +20.4% on the same period. China momentum requires more than twice the lookback window to find alpha, and even then yields less than half the risk-adjusted return.

III. Lookback Period

This is the most important parameter in China. Unlike the US, where 6-month momentum dominates, short lookbacks in China do not just underperform — they actively destroy capital. The 6-month configuration loses −10.9% per year. The signal only turns positive around 12 months and reaches its peak at 22 months.

Why do short lookbacks fail in China? China A-shares exhibit strong short-term mean reversion. Stocks that have risen sharply in the past 3–9 months tend to revert. This is consistent with documented retail investor behaviour on Chinese exchanges — momentum chasers push prices above fair value over short horizons, creating a reversion effect. The signal only becomes exploitable at horizons long enough to capture fundamental trends rather than speculative noise.

IV. The Regime Filter Is Non-Negotiable

The most striking finding is what happens when the MA150 regime filter is removed. Without it, every configuration — even the best 22-month momentum — collapses.

22m-1, top 7 — WITH MA150
CAGR: +12.4% | Sharpe: 0.35 | MaxDD: -52.7%
Invested: 49% of months
22m-1, top 7 — WITHOUT regime
CAGR: -1.4% | Sharpe: -0.09 | MaxDD: -89.1%
Invested: 95% of months

Without the regime filter, the strategy loses money and draws down nearly 90%. China's equity market is highly cyclical, punctuated by violent bull and bear markets driven by policy, liquidity, and retail sentiment. Staying invested during bear regimes erases all momentum gains. The MA150 filter forces the strategy into cash during the 2010–2012, 2015–2016, and 2021–2024 downturns — exactly when momentum stocks suffered the most.

VI. Annual Returns

The strategy's returns are highly concentrated in specific years. The 2013–2015 bull market produced outsized gains; 2023–2024 were difficult years as the strategy held small-cap momentum stocks during a drawdown in that segment.

2023–2024 were the worst two consecutive years since inception. 2023: −21.7% strategy vs −11.4% CSI 300. 2024: −10.3% strategy vs +14.7% CSI 300. This highlights a key risk: momentum in small/mid-caps can disconnect from the broader market, especially during policy-driven rotations into large-caps (which dominated 2024 in China). The lack of a market cap filter exacerbates this.

Despite weaker standalone metrics, China momentum offers one critical advantage: near-zero correlation with US momentum. The value of China momentum may lie not in its standalone alpha, but in its diversification properties within a multi-market portfolio.

VIII. Limitations & Open Questions

Data integrity

Historical total shares (used for market cap calculation) are only available through October 2024. Post-October data uses forward-filled values, meaning the market cap filter for 2025–2026 is approximate. Capital actions (secondary offerings, buybacks) after that date are not reflected.

Survivorship bias

The price dataset includes delisted tickers, which partially mitigates survivorship bias. However, the completeness of delisted stock coverage in the source data has not been independently verified.

Transaction costs

No transaction costs are modelled. In China, stamp duty (0.05% one-way) and brokerage commissions apply. For a top-7 portfolio with monthly rebalance, estimated round-trip costs of ~0.3–0.5% per month would reduce annual returns by approximately 3–5 percentage points.

Market access

Not all A-shares are accessible to foreign investors through Stock Connect. Stocks below approximately ¥50B market cap are typically not included. Testing with a ¥50B minimum cap significantly reduces the universe and lowers Sharpe to ~0.21, with CAGR around +6.8%. The stronger numbers above reflect the full universe including potentially inaccessible stocks.

No quality filter

The strategy has no profitability or fundamental filter. Adding an ROE or earnings quality screen could reduce exposure to "junk momentum" — stocks with high past returns but deteriorating fundamentals. This is an active area of ongoing research.
