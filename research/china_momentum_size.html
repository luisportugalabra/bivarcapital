China MomentumWhy Size Inverts Everything

In the US, momentum gets stronger as market cap increases — peaking at +20.4% CAGR in mega caps. In China, the opposite is true. We divide 5,444 A-shares into five market cap quintiles and run 21m-1 momentum independently in each. The result is unambiguous: alpha disappears monotonically as size increases. Large caps lose money. Micro caps compound at +15% per year.

In the US, small-cap momentum is polluted by speculative stocks that spike temporarily with no fundamental basis. Filtering to large caps eliminates the noise and leaves only stocks where genuine institutional flows drive sustained trends.

China runs the opposite logic in every dimension.

IV. Why Large Caps Fail in China

State-owned enterprises dominate the top quintile

Q5 mega-caps (average ¥549亿, ~$7.5B USD) are dominated by state-owned enterprises: the four largest banks, PetroChina, CNOOC, China Telecom, SAIC Motor. These companies do not respond to momentum signals. Their share prices move on policy announcements, government capital injections, and regulatory decisions — not earnings momentum or competitive dynamics. Buying them when they've been going up is not momentum investing; it is betting on continued policy support.

Q4 large caps are the worst of both worlds

Q4 (average ¥86亿, ~$1.2B USD) is neither large enough to benefit from institutional flows nor small enough to exhibit the retail-neglect dynamic that drives micro-cap momentum. These stocks underperform the CSI 300 by −6.5% per year when selected by 21-month momentum. They are the most dangerous quintile in this study.

Analyst coverage drops dramatically below ¥50亿

The bottom two quintiles (Q1 and Q2, below ~¥30亿) have near-zero institutional analyst coverage. When a company in this range improves its fundamentals — revenue growth, margin expansion, contract wins — the price adjustment is slow and persistent. The momentum signal at 21 months captures this delayed repricing. By the time the stock reaches Q3 or above, institutional analysts have covered it, the fast money has traded it, and the momentum signal is gone.

China momentum is structural neglect, not institutional flows. The alpha comes from buying unloved small companies where price discovery is slow. As soon as a company gets big enough to attract analyst coverage and institutional attention, the edge disappears.

V. The Practical Catch

Q1 micro (average ¥17亿, ~$235M USD) sits below the formal Stock Connect eligibility threshold, which requires approximately ¥60亿 market cap for Shanghai-listed stocks. This means most of these stocks are not accessible via IBKR's standard Stock Connect interface.

However, platforms with broader A-share access — Moomoo, Futu — can execute in this size range. We verified this for the current 21m-1 portfolio: all 10 top-ranked micro-cap stocks as of May 2026 are Shanghai-listed (SSE 6xxxxx series) and accessible through Moomoo with standard commissions.

Transaction costs in micro caps are meaningful. Spread + commission on ¥17亿 stocks averages ~0.3–0.5% per side. At monthly rebalancing with typical 30–40% turnover, this costs ~2–3% per year — reducing the +15.3% gross CAGR to approximately +12–13% net. Still well above the CSI 300.

VI. Key Findings

1 · The relationship is monotonic

Sharpe falls from 0.46 (Q1) to −0.11 (Q4), then recovers slightly to 0.07 (Q5 mega). This is not noise — the pattern is consistent across the full 17-year test period and across different lookback windows. Larger is worse, except at the very top where policy effects provide a floor.

2 · Large caps actively destroy value

Q4 loses −6.5% per year versus the CSI 300. Applying the standard US filter of "large caps only" would eliminate all alpha in the China dataset and replace it with significant underperformance. This is the single most important difference from US momentum research.

3 · Q1 micro has the best risk-adjusted return in this study

Sharpe 0.46, Sortino 0.97, Max DD −39%. The drawdown is dramatically lower than any other quintile — Q2 through Q4 all draw down 62–75%. This is counter-intuitive: the smallest, least-liquid stocks have the lowest drawdown when selected by momentum. The regime filter (go to cash when CSI 300 is below MA150) protects the portfolio during the market crashes that devastate the larger quintiles.

4 · Q5 mega partially works — for a different reason

Q5 (¥549亿 average) slightly outperforms Q4 at +4.0% CAGR. This is likely not momentum but policy mean-reversion: when large SOEs have been going up for 21 months, it sometimes reflects genuine policy tailwinds that persist. But at Sharpe 0.07, it is barely distinguishable from the benchmark.
