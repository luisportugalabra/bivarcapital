MOMENTUM STRATEGY — FULL SPECIFICATION
========================================
Bivar Capital · April 2026


OVERVIEW
--------
Long-only momentum strategy on US large-cap equities with a profitability
filter and market regime overlay. Monthly rebalance.


STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION
-----------------------

1. TIMING
   Execute on the last trading day of each month, after market close.
   Use closing prices for all calculations and (theoretical) execution.

2. REGIME CHECK — S&P 500 vs MA200
   Calculate the 200-day simple moving average of the S&P 500 closing price.
   Compare today's S&P 500 close to the MA200:

   - S&P 500 CLOSE > MA200 → INVESTED (proceed to step 3)
   - S&P 500 CLOSE ≤ MA200 → CASH (sell everything, hold cash, skip to step 7)

   This is binary. Check this FIRST, before any stock selection.

3. UNIVERSE
   Start with all US domestic common stocks with:
   - Market capitalisation > $5 billion (as of most recent quarterly report)

   This gives roughly 500-700 stocks at any given time.

4. PROFITABILITY FILTER
   Remove any stock with EBIT ≤ 0 over the trailing twelve months (TTM).

   EBIT = Earnings Before Interest and Taxes (operating profit).
   TTM = sum of the last 4 reported quarters.

   Use data with a 2-month availability lag: if a company's fiscal quarter
   ends March 31, assume the data is only available from June 1 onwards.
   This prevents look-ahead bias.

5. MOMENTUM SIGNAL — 9-1
   For each remaining stock, calculate:

   MOMENTUM = (Price 1 month ago) / (Price 9 months ago) - 1

   This is the total return over 8 months, measured from 9 months ago to
   1 month ago. The most recent month is SKIPPED because of short-term
   reversal effects.

   Example (rebalancing on June 30, 2025):
   - Price on September 30, 2024 (9 months ago)
   - Price on May 31, 2025 (1 month ago)
   - Momentum = May price / September price - 1

6. PORTFOLIO CONSTRUCTION
   - Rank all stocks by momentum signal, highest to lowest
   - Select the TOP 20
   - Allocate EQUAL WEIGHT to each: 5% per stock (1/20)
   - Buy at the closing price

7. HOLD
   Hold the portfolio for one month. Do nothing until the next rebalance date.

8. REPEAT
   On the last trading day of the next month, go back to step 2.


REBALANCE MECHANICS
-------------------
On each rebalance:
- Check MA200 first
- If switching from INVESTED to CASH: sell all positions
- If switching from CASH to INVESTED: buy new top 20
- If staying INVESTED: sell stocks that dropped out of top 20, buy new entries,
  rebalance remaining positions to equal weight
- If staying CASH: do nothing

Typical turnover: ~5-10 trades per month (not all 20 change every month).


WHAT THIS STRATEGY DOES NOT DO
-------------------------------
- No shorting
- No leverage
- No derivatives or options
- No sector constraints (momentum naturally concentrates in leading sectors)
- No market cap weighting (equal weight only)
- No discretionary overrides
- No fundamental analysis beyond EBIT > 0


PARAMETERS (FIXED)
------------------
Lookback:           9 months
Skip:               1 month (most recent)
Portfolio size:     20 stocks
Minimum market cap: $5 billion
Profitability:      EBIT > 0 (TTM)
Regime indicator:   S&P 500 200-day simple moving average
Regime rule:        Above = invested, Below = 100% cash
Weighting:          Equal weight
Rebalance:          Monthly (last trading day)


WHY THESE PARAMETERS
---------------------
- 9-month lookback: tested 6, 9, 12. 9-month produced the best Sharpe ratio.
  12-month signal is too stale; 6-month is too noisy with the MA200 filter.

- Skip last month: academic consensus (Jegadeesh & Titman 1993). The most
  recent month exhibits short-term reversal that contaminates the momentum signal.

- Top 20: tested 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. Top 20 has the best risk-adjusted
  returns (highest Sharpe). Fewer = more concentration risk. More = diluted signal.

- Equal weight: tested inverse-volatility weighting. It underperformed because
  it underweights the most volatile (= highest momentum) stocks.

- EBIT > 0: tested ROIC > median, ROIC > p75. Additional quality filters
  HURT performance by removing legitimate momentum winners in capital-intensive
  industries. EBIT > 0 is the minimum filter that removes unprofitable hype stocks.

- MA200: tested MA150, MA200. MA200 is standard and well-documented. Going to
  100% cash (vs 50%) produces better Sharpe but slightly lower CAGR. Both work.

- Monthly rebalance: tested quarterly and annual. Annual kills the strategy
  (CAGR drops from +16.5% to +8.7%). Momentum decays fast.

COSTS NOT INCLUDED
------------------
- Transaction costs: estimated 1-2% annual drag (net CAGR ~14.5-15.5%)
- Taxes on dividends: ~0.15% drag (negligible — momentum stocks have low yields)
- Taxes on capital gains: 3-5% drag in taxable accounts (monthly rebalance =
  mostly short-term gains). Strategy is best suited for tax-advantaged accounts.
- Slippage: minimal for stocks > $5B

RISKS
-----
1. Momentum crashes: factor rotations where momentum underperforms while the
   broad market rises (2021: +3% vs S&P +22%). MA200 does NOT protect against this.

2. Sector concentration: momentum concentrates in whatever sector leads the market.
   AI/tech in 2024-2025. Energy in 2022. A sector-specific shock is amplified.

3. Regime filter lag: MA200 is always late. Fast crashes happen before it triggers
   (Q4 2018). Recoveries see delayed re-entry (2009, 2023).

4. Recent outliers: 2024 (+73%) is extreme. Do not extrapolate.
