Strategy Rules

  1. Universe: LSE-listed UK common stocks (GBX-denominated), market cap > £250M, TTM EBIT > 0.
  2. Signal: Rank all qualifying stocks by 6-month total return. Select the top 7.
  3. Portfolio: Equal weight — approximately 14.3% per position.
  4. Rebalance: First business day of each calendar month.

Regime Filter

When the FTSE 100 closes below its 200-day moving average, the strategy exits all positions and holds cash until the index recovers above that level. This dampens drawdowns during sustained bear markets without requiring a market-timing forecast.

The regime filter was active for 102 of 306 months (33%) over the backtest period — including the 2002–2003 downturn, the 2008–2009 financial crisis, and the 2020 Covid shock. Max drawdown with the filter: -33.4% vs. a deeper unfiltered decline.

Universe

The investable universe is restricted to LSE-listed UK common stocks priced in GBX (pence). Two screens are applied at each monthly rebalance:

  • Market cap > £250M — excludes micro- and small-cap stocks where liquidity is limited.
  • TTM EBIT > 0 — requires trailing twelve-month operating profitability; filters out loss-making companies.
  • Common stock only — no REITs, investment trusts, or foreign listings.

Correlation with US Optimal Momentum

The UK strategy has a +0.28 monthly return correlation with the US Optimal Momentum strategy — low enough to provide meaningful diversification.

A 50/50 combination of the two strategies raises the blended Sharpe ratio from ~0.92 to ~1.25 and reduces peak-to-trough drawdown from -33% to -24%, with no sacrifice in expected return.

Limitations

All results are backtested and do not represent live trading. Past performance does not guarantee future results. The universe includes delisted stocks to partially mitigate survivorship bias, but look-ahead bias and transaction cost assumptions may affect real-world outcomes. Concentrated portfolios of 7 stocks carry significant idiosyncratic risk. This report is for informational purposes only and does not constitute investment advice.
