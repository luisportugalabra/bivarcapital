Baidu (NASDAQ: BIDU)The ice cube is melting. The iceberg underneath is free.

But net cash covers roughly 80% of the market cap, leaving an enterprise value of ~$7B for: China's #2 AI cloud growing 50%, the world's most international robotaxi operation, and one of the only working Nvidia alternatives inside China.

Investment Thesis

Baidu is two companies wearing one ticker. Company A is a declining search-ads franchise: online marketing revenue of RMB 13.1B fell -19% YoY as Chinese users migrate queries to AI chatbots — including Baidu's own. Company B is an AI infrastructure complex: AI Cloud infra +50% YoY (GPU cloud +283%), the Kunlun chip line selling into a China desperate for non-Nvidia compute, and Apollo Go — 350M+ autonomous miles, operating in 28 cities including London, Dubai, Hong Kong and Switzerland.

The market prices the blend as a value trap: ~$34B market cap for a company holding ~$26B in net cash and earning ~$1.5B/yr non-GAAP even mid-transition. Strip the cash and you pay ~5× earnings for the AI businesses plus the robotaxi option. AI-powered revenue is already 50% of Baidu Core ex-ads-decline — this is no longer a story about whether the mix shifts. It is shifting. The question is only whether shareholders ever see the cash.

At ~$7-8B of enterprise value, you are paying roughly one year of OpenAI's compute bill for China's #2 AI cloud, its leading robotaxi platform, and a working domestic AI chip. The ads decline is real — and it is also the only reason this price exists.

Bull Points

01 — The Balance Sheet Is Most of the Price

RMB 283B (~$41.7B) in cash, deposits and investments against ~$15.3B of total debt: ~$26B net cash vs a $33.7B market cap. Even applying a harsh 40-50% "China cash discount", the operating business trades at a low-teens billion valuation while producing positive operating income (RMB 3.8B non-GAAP in Q2 alone) through the worst ads quarter in its history.

02 — The AI Mix Shift Is Real and Measured

AI-powered business revenue hit RMB 12.5B in Q2, +25% YoY, now 50% of core general business: AI Cloud infra RMB 7.3B (+50%), GPU cloud +283%, AI applications RMB 2.5B, AI-native marketing RMB 2.6B. Within roughly two quarters the growing half of Baidu becomes larger than the shrinking half — the headline revenue number inflects mechanically, and screens start showing a growth company again.

03 — Apollo Go: The Most International Robotaxi Company on Earth

350M+ cumulative autonomous miles, 28 cities, and the only operator running driverless programs simultaneously in China, the Middle East and Europe (London, Dubai, Hong Kong, Switzerland). Waymo is bigger in the US and worth $45B+ in private markets. Apollo Go is carried inside Baidu at, effectively, zero.

04 — Kunlun: Scarcity Pricing for Domestic AI Compute

Export controls made non-Nvidia AI compute a strategic necessity in China. Kunlunxin is one of a handful of working alternatives, with reported multi-billion-RMB orders and a public product roadmap. Baidu is both its own anchor customer (the GPU cloud growing 283% runs partly on it) and an emerging merchant supplier into a market with essentially captive demand.

05 — HK Dual-Primary Listing → Southbound Flows

Conversion to dual-primary status in Hong Kong is expected within 2026, making BIDU eligible for Stock Connect. Mainland investors — who see Apollo Go on their streets and cannot buy the ADR — get access. For comparable conversions this has been a durable, mechanical source of demand.

06 — Still Profitable Through the Trough

This is not a cash-burning turnaround: Q2 non-GAAP operating income RMB 3.8B, non-GAAP net income RMB 2.6B, operating cash flow RMB 3.4B. Baidu funds the robotaxi expansion and chip R&D from its own P&L while ads melt. Few "melting ice cube" stocks self-fund their second act.

Risks & Bear Points

01

The cash may never reach you. Buybacks of just $259M since Q1 — under 2% of market cap annualized — against $26B of net cash. Most of it sits onshore in RMB inside a VIE structure. If capital never returns to shareholders, the "net cash floor" is theoretical and the market is right to discount it.

02

Ads decline could accelerate past the AI offset. -19% YoY is not a fade, it's a cliff. If chatbot substitution (Doubao, DeepSeek, WeChat AI) keeps compressing search faster than cloud grows, total revenue keeps shrinking and the transition narrative dies before the mix flips.

03

AI cloud economics are unproven in a price war. Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance and the carriers all subsidize AI compute. GPU cloud +283% says nothing about gross margin; Chinese cloud has historically destroyed capital.

04

Robotaxi is a capital furnace with regulatory beta. Scaling 28 cities of driverless operations costs billions before unit economics are proven, and a single high-profile accident anywhere resets timelines everywhere.

05

Structural China discount: VIE, ADR delisting risk, geopolitics. None of it is new, all of it is real, and it caps the multiple this will ever trade at. The exit likely comes from cash returns or the HK line, not from a US re-rating.

06

iQIYI consolidation muddies everything. RMB 6.3B of declining, low-margin video revenue and part of that consolidated cash belongs to iQIYI's minority holders — SOTP math needs a haircut for it.

Verdict

You are not buying the search business. You are buying $26B of cash and three AI lottery tickets for $34B — while the market stares at the one line item that's dying.

DEEP VALUE WITH OPTIONALITY · CHINA-SIZED POSITION ONLY · THESIS BREAKS IF ADS DECAY OUTRUNS CLOUD GROWTH THROUGH 2027

Baidu is cheaper than Coupang on every metric and riskier on every dimension that matters: governance, capital return, jurisdiction. It is a bet that arithmetic eventually beats narrative — that a profitable company trading near net cash, with its growth segment at 50% of core and compounding at 25%+, does not stay at a ~$7B enterprise value. Size it like what it is: a mispriced option, not a compounder.
