What travels is the protection, not the alpha. Max drawdown falls from -76/-96% to -30/-60% on every single coin — the trend + volatility filter is a universal brake. But excess return only exists where trends are slow and persistent. On event-driven coins (DOGE, XRP, LINK) the signal is systematically late to vertical rallies, and the crashes outrun the MA155.

04 — Correlation & portfolio construction

Strategy-return correlations with the BTC signal are genuinely moderate — BNB +0.37, SOL +0.40, ETH +0.50, AVAX +0.28 — which is exactly why the portfolio question deserved a real answer. Diversification only helps if the legs carry edge:

Every blend is worse than BTC alone on every metric. Moderate correlation cannot rescue legs whose edge evaporates out of sample — that is dilution, not diversification.

Verdict

The signal does not travel — and nothing else found in 10 other coins deserves capital. The production signal's edge is specific to Bitcoin's slow, persistent trend regimes. Applied elsewhere it degrades to second-division results at best; coin-specific optimization produces spectacular backtests that collapse 51-110% the moment they are evaluated blind.

The book stays BTC-only. This is not a preference — it is the unanimous answer of three independent tests: one config across 11 coins, per-coin parameter sweeps, and multi-family strategy iteration, all settled out of sample. One honest corollary for BTC itself: a machine that earns 0.7-1.0 "away from home" argues for anchoring live expectations at Sharpe ≈1.0-1.3, consistent with the 3-year-block analysis in the companion report.

Methodology: signals on closed daily candles, t+1 execution, 10bps/order on every position change, no leverage. Warm-up periods excluded per coin. Train/test split at 2023-12-31 with champions selected strictly on training data. Correlations computed on daily strategy returns, common window.
