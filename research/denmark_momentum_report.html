XV. Risks & Caveats

  • Transaction costs included. 25bps round-trip applied on actual monthly turnover. Represents bid-ask spread for liquid Danish mid/large caps on Nasdaq Copenhagen.
  • Backtest overfitting risk. MA100, N=10, 6M+12M momentum and mcap floor were selected from sensitivity analysis. Expect 20–30% Sharpe degradation in live trading.
  • Benchmark is synthetic. Equal-weighted index of all stocks >700M DKK — not the OMXC25 index. Includes dividends via adjusted prices.
  • Concentration risk. 10 stocks, InvVol weighted. Novo Nordisk dominates Danish market cap — our universe includes it but weights by volatility, not mcap.
  • Data source: EODHD adjusted prices & market cap. Adjusted for splits, dividends, and corporate actions.
  • Realistic live expectation: CAGR 18–21%, Sharpe 1.2–1.43.
