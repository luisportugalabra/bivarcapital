Monthly vs Annual Rebalance

Does monthly rebalancing justify the extra costs? At >$5B market cap, monthly rebalance adds +0.9% to +1.4% CAGR over annual rebalance. With 25 stocks and ~40% monthly turnover, transaction costs are roughly 0.5% per year at institutional rates. Net benefit: approximately +0.5% to +1.0% per year — meaningful over decades but not transformative.

Strategy Descriptions

Mom 12-1
Return from 12 months ago to 1 month ago. Skip the most recent month to avoid short-term reversal. The academic standard.
Mom 6-1
Return from 6 months ago to 1 month ago. Shorter window captures more recent trends. Works best with monthly rebalancing.
Mom 12-2
Return from 12 months ago to 2 months ago. Skip last 2 months. Used in some implementations for additional reversal protection.
Intermediate (Novy-Marx)
Return from 12 months ago to 6 months ago. Uses only the "intermediate" period. Designed to avoid momentum crashes but loses signal with frequent rebalancing.
Implementation
Universe refreshed annually from Sharadar fundamentals. Market cap filter applied at time of ranking. End-of-month adjusted close prices used for momentum signal and return calculation. Dividends included via adjusted prices. No sector exclusions (momentum is price-based, sector-neutral). 25 stocks equal weight. Rebalance on last trading day of each month.
