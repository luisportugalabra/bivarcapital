The Rules

BUY when all three are true:
1. RSI(14) > 54
2. Price > 155-day Moving Average
3. 20-day Annualised Volatility < 100%

SELL (100% cash) when any condition fails. Check daily at close.

RSI(14) > 54
Confirms upward momentum is present. RSI above 54 means recent gains outweigh losses. Filters out sideways and early-stage declines.
Price > MA155
Confirms the long-term trend is bullish. Below the 155-day average = bear market. The strategy sits in cash during sustained downtrends.
Volatility < 100%
Removes extreme-volatility periods where even bullish trends become unpredictable. Avoids the chaotic days around crashes and capitulation events.
Why it works
Bitcoin trends for months then crashes 60–80%. These three rules identify the trending periods and avoid the crashes. Simple trend-following on a trending asset.

Caveats

Overfitting risk
RSI threshold (54) and MA period (155) are optimised on historical data. Expect Sharpe degradation of 30–50% in live trading. Realistic estimate: Sharpe 1.2–1.5.
Execution
Strategy assumes execution at daily close. Slippage on BTC is minimal for retail-sized positions (<$1M) on major exchanges.
Regime change
BTC may stop trending as it matures and institutional adoption increases. If BTC becomes range-bound, trend-following will underperform.
Tax
Frequent trading (enters/exits ~20 times per year) generates short-term capital gains events.
