II. The Rules

BUY when all three are true:
1. RSI(14) > 54
2. Price > 155-day Simple Moving Average
3. 20-day Annualised Volatility < 100%

SELL (100% cash) when any condition fails. Check daily at close.

RSI(14) > 54
Confirms short-term momentum. RSI measures the ratio of recent gains to losses over 14 days. Above 54 means more up days than down — trend is intact.
Price > MA155
Confirms long-term bullish trend. The 155-day simple moving average is the average price over ~7 months. Below it = bear market. The strategy sits in cash during sustained downtrends.
Volatility < 100%
Removes extreme chaos. Annualised volatility above 100% signals panic or capitulation. Even in bull markets, these days are unpredictable. The filter keeps you out until things calm down.
Why it works
Bitcoin trends for months then crashes 60–80%. These rules identify trending periods and avoid crashes. The MA155 catches bear markets, the RSI catches corrections within bulls, the vol filter catches flash crashes.

VII. What the Strategy Misses

Late entries
When BTC recovers from a bear, the price must cross above MA155 and RSI must exceed 54. This misses the first 20–30% of recovery. In 2023, the strategy captured +72% vs +155% for buy & hold.
Bull run tail ends
In 2017, BTC rose +1,369%. The strategy captured +868% — still extraordinary but not the full move. The RSI and MA filters exit before the absolute peak.
Whipsaw periods
In choppy sideways markets, the RSI oscillates around 54 causing frequent small entries and exits. Each round trip has execution cost.
Regime change risk
Bitcoin may stop trending as it matures. If BTC becomes range-bound like gold, trend-following will underperform.

VIII. Caveats

Overfitting
RSI threshold (54) and MA period (155) are optimised on 11 years of data. Expect Sharpe degradation of 30–50% in live trading. Realistic estimate: Sharpe 1.2–1.5.
Execution
Backtest assumes execution at daily close. In practice, you check signals after close and execute next morning. Slippage is minimal for retail-sized BTC positions.
Taxes (Portugal)
Crypto held < 365 days: 28% flat tax on gains. The strategy trades ~20 times per year, so most gains are short-term. Net CAGR after tax: ~63%.
Trading frequency
~20 entries/exits per year. At 0.1% per trade (Binance), annual cost is ~2% — negligible vs the CAGR.
