Section X

Caveats

Short history. 11 years. BTC may change as institutional adoption grows.

Overfitting. Parameters optimised on this data. Expect 20–30% Sharpe degradation.

Costs. ~22 trades/yr × 0.2% = ~4%/yr drag. Not in backtest.

Cash drag. In cash 66% of the time at 0% yield.

Section XI

Methodology

Data Source
CoinMetrics Community API — BTC reference rate (daily, 00:00 UTC)
Period
January 2015 – April 2026 (11.3 years)
RSI Calculation
SMA-based RSI (14-day rolling mean of gains/losses)
Moving Average
155-day Simple Moving Average
Volatility
20-day std × √365 (annualised)
Execution
Signal at close → execute same close (BTC 24/7). Costs not included.
