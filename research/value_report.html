Value Composite 1, 2 & 3Quarterly TTM fundamentals. Monthly and annual rebalance.

Three versions of O'Shaughnessy's Value Composite tested with quarterly trailing-twelve-month fundamentals on 21 years of survivorship-bias-free data (2005–2026). VC1 uses 5 pure valuation factors. VC2 adds shareholder yield. VC3 uses dividend yield instead.

VC2 >$200M monthly: +12.1% CAGR  ·  VC3 >$5B monthly: +11.8% CAGR
Quarterly TTM data · 25 stocks equal weight · S&P 500: +8.4%

The Three Composites

VC1 (5 factors)
P/E + P/S + P/B + P/FCF + EV/EBITDA
Pure valuation, no yield component
VC2 (6 factors)
VC1 + Shareholder Yield (dividends + buybacks TTM)
The original O'Shaughnessy composite
VC3 (6 factors)
VC1 + Dividend Yield only
Simpler yield measure, no buyback component
Data
All metrics use TTM (trailing 4 quarters) for flow items. P/B uses latest quarter book value. Refreshed monthly.

Key findings: At >$200M, VC2 (with shareholder yield) is best (+12.1% monthly). At >$5B, VC3 (with dividend yield) wins (+11.8%). Monthly rebalance outperforms annual significantly at >$200M but is roughly equal at >$5B. All three composites beat the S&P 500 across both market caps with quarterly TTM data — a meaningful improvement over the annual fundamentals results.
