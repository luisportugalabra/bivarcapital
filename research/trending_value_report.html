Trending Value 1, 2 & 3Value + 6-month momentum. Quarterly TTM.

The Trending Value strategy combines value screening with momentum: take the cheapest 20% by value composite, then buy the 25 with the strongest 6-month price momentum. Tested with three value composites (VC1, VC2, VC3) on quarterly TTM data, 2005–2026.

TV3 >$5B monthly: +13.8% CAGR  ·  +5.4% alpha  ·  Sharpe 0.82
VC3 (P/E + P/S + P/B + P/FCF + EV/EBITDA + Div Yield) + Momentum 6-1 · 25 stocks

Adding momentum to value improves every metric. Compared to pure value composites: CAGR increases by +1–2%, Sharpe improves from 0.5–0.6 to 0.7–0.8, and max drawdown decreases by 10–20 percentage points. The momentum filter avoids value traps — cheap stocks still falling — by only buying cheap stocks with positive price trends.

How It Works

Step 1
Rank all stocks by value composite (VC1, VC2, or VC3). Take the cheapest 20%.
Step 2
From that pool, calculate 6-month price momentum (return from t−6 to t−1, skipping most recent month).
Step 3
Buy the top 25 by momentum. Equal weight. Hold until next rebalance.

Methodology

Value screen
VC1: P/E, P/S, P/B, P/FCF, EV/EBITDA
VC2: VC1 + Shareholder Yield (TTM)
VC3: VC1 + Dividend Yield
Momentum
6-month price return, skip most recent month
Selection
Cheapest 20% by value → top 25 by momentum
Data
Sharadar quarterly TTM. Refreshed monthly. 2-month availability lag.
Period
2005–2026 · Survivorship-bias free
Rebalance
Monthly and annual (January) tested
Portfolio
25 stocks, equal weight
