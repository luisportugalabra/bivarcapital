XV. Risks & Caveats

  • Transaction costs included. 20bps round-trip on actual monthly turnover. Conservative estimate for TSX liquid mid/large caps.
  • Backtest overfitting risk. MA75, N=10, 12-1 momentum and mcap/ADV floors were selected from sensitivity analysis. Expect some Sharpe degradation in live trading.
  • Benchmark is TSX Composite (^GSPTSE). Price return only (no dividends). Strategy uses adjusted prices including dividends — slight benchmark understatement.
  • Resource concentration. TSX is heavily weighted in energy and materials. Top 10 momentum stocks may be sector-concentrated in commodity cycles.
  • Data source: EODHD adjusted prices & market cap (shares × price). Adjusted for splits, dividends, and corporate actions.
  • Realistic live expectation: CAGR 27–32%, Sharpe 0.93–1.18.
