I. Strategy Specification

Signal Day: Last trading day of each month (after market close)

  1. Regime check: Is the S&P 500 close above its 250-day simple moving average?
    • NO → Sell all stocks. Buy GLD (SPDR Gold Shares). Hold until next month’s signal day.
    • YES → Proceed to step 2.
  2. Compute momentum for every eligible stock:
    Momentum = 50% × (price / price 126 trading days ago − 1) + 50% × (price / price 252 trading days ago − 1)
  3. Filter universe:
    • Market capitalisation > $10 billion
    • EBIT > 0 (operating profit positive, most recent report)
    • US domestic common stock only
  4. Select top 7 by momentum composite, with a maximum of 3 stocks per sector.
  5. Execute on first trading day of next month: sell positions that left the portfolio, buy new ones. Equal weight (~14.3% each).
Why momentum works: Stocks that have risen tend to continue rising. Institutional capital flows are slow — funds take weeks to build positions. Information propagates gradually. Behavioural biases cause investors to sell winners too early and hold losers too long.
Why GLD during bear markets: Gold has near-zero correlation with the momentum strategy (0.015 over 21 years). During bear markets, gold averages +1.5%/month while stocks fall. The MA250 regime filter identifies bear markets early — switching to GLD preserves capital and generates positive returns during drawdowns.

VIII. Why Each Parameter

Momentum blend: 50% × 6-month + 50% × 12-month

Combining two horizons captures both short-term acceleration and medium-term trend. Tested against pure 6m, 9m, 12m, and multiple weighted blends. This combination delivered the highest Sharpe ratio with daily data and realistic execution.

Market cap > $10B

Below $10B, momentum captures noise — pump-and-dumps, illiquid stocks, micro-caps with unreliable data. Above $10B, momentum reflects genuine institutional capital flows and earnings-driven re-ratings. The $8–12B range was tested; $10B is the sweet spot.

EBIT > 0

Filters out money-losing companies. Without this filter, momentum picks up hyped stocks with no fundamentals. Removing the EBIT filter reduces Sharpe by 0.10–0.15 in testing.

Max 3 per sector

Without sector limits, the portfolio frequently holds 5–6 technology stocks. A tech correction hits all positions simultaneously. The cap of 3 forces diversification, reducing drawdown by ~5 percentage points with minimal CAGR cost.

MA250 regime filter

When the S&P 500 closes below its 250-day moving average, the strategy exits all stock positions. Without this filter, maximum drawdown exceeds −60%. With it, drawdown is contained to −36.2%. The MA250 is slower than MA200, which reduces whipsaws (false exits and re-entries).

GLD during bear months

Gold has a correlation of 0.015 with the momentum strategy over the full sample. During the 51 bear months, GLD averaged +1.52%/month. Holding GLD instead of cash adds approximately +4% CAGR without materially increasing drawdown.

Signal: last trading day, execution: first trading day of next month

Tested all trading days of the month (1st, 5th, 10th, 15th, 18th, 20th, 25th, last). The last trading day produced the highest Sharpe ratio (1.10) and lowest MaxDD (−36.2%). Execution on the first trading day of the next month ensures no look-ahead bias — the signal is computed after market close on the last day, and trades are placed the following trading day.

XIII. Risks & Caveats

  • Transaction costs not included. Approximately 84 stock trades per year plus ~4 GLD rotations. Estimated impact: −0.5% to −1.0% CAGR.
  • Overfitting risk. Multiple parameter combinations were tested. Expect 20–30% Sharpe degradation in live trading.
  • 21 years, 2 bear markets. Both were V-shaped recoveries (2008, 2020). A slow bear market (2000–2003 style) may behave differently.
  • 2024 dominates recent returns. The strategy returned +188.9% in 2024 driven by SMCI, APP, COIN, VRT. Without this outlier year, CAGR is approximately +22%.
  • Concentration risk. 7 stocks at 14.3% each. A single-stock blowup costs ~14% of the portfolio.
  • Realistic expectation post-costs: CAGR +22–26%, Sharpe 0.75–0.90.
