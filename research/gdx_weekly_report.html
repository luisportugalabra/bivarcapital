The Signal

# Every Friday at market close:
gdx_weekly_return = GDX[today] / GDX[last_friday] − 1

IF gdx_weekly_return > +2%:
    # Enter Monday open, exit next Friday close
    BUY Gold futures (GC) + Silver futures (SI)  # 50 / 50

ELSE:
    CASH  # ~65% of all weeks

Why it works: GDX is a NYSE-listed equity ETF — it absorbs institutional orders, analyst upgrades, and macro positioning first. Gold and Silver futures react slower. The weekly signal has an out-of-sample Sharpe of 1.25 (second half 2016–2026), nearly identical to the in-sample 1.20 (2006–2016). No parameter optimisation other than the >2% threshold, which works at any positive value.

Regime filter (optional): The signal works significantly better when Gold is above its 200-day moving average (Sharpe 1.55 vs 0.16 in bear markets). Applying this filter as a gate reduces drawdown further at a small cost to CAGR.

Caveats & Risks

Short history. 20 years covers only one full gold cycle. The relationship between GDX and physical metals may change as market structure evolves.

Regime dependence. Sharpe 1.55 when Gold is above MA200, but only 0.16 in bear markets. The strategy is implicitly long the gold bull market cycle.

Silver volatility. Silver weekly vol ~3%. A -8% to -10% week in Silver is possible and will occur during the signal. Position sizing must account for this.

Signal arbitrage. As this relationship becomes known, it may weaken. Monitor rolling 2yr Sharpe annually.

Cash drag. In cash 65% of the time at 0% (or T-bill yield). Combine with other uncorrelated strategies (e.g. Bivar Optimal Momentum, Overnight Commodities) to deploy capital efficiently.
