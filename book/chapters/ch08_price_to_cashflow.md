# Chapter 8 — Price-to-Cash Flow

## What the Book Claims (1964–2009)
- D1 All Stocks: **16.25% CAGR**, $10,000 → $10,187,545
- Sharpe: **0.61** vs 0.33 universe
- Base rates: 91% rolling 5yr, **100% rolling 10yr**
- "Uniformly high base rates"

## What We Found (2005–2026, Quarterly TTM)

| | D1 | D10 | Spread | vs Universe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| >$200M | +9.9% | +10.2% | **-0.4%** | -1.0% |
| >$5B | +11.4% | +10.1% | **+1.2%** | -0.1% |

Rolling 5yr positive: 76% (>$200M), 65% (>$5B)

## Verdict
**Marginal.** The factor that beat the universe 100% of rolling 10-year periods in the book now produces a near-zero or slightly negative spread. At >$5B there's a thin +1.2% spread but far from the 5%+ the book reported. P/CF (using FCF as proxy) has lost most of its predictive power.
