## Verdict
**Inverted at >$200M. Dead at >$5B.**

At >$200M, high dividend yield stocks underperform no-dividend stocks by 2.9% per year. The D10 (low/no dividend) stocks are dominated by tech and growth — the biggest winners of the last 15 years.

At >$5B the spread is barely negative (-0.5%) — essentially zero. Dividend yield has gone from a modest alpha generator to noise.

The book found "modest" alpha even in its sample. Our data shows that modest edge has disappeared entirely.
