# Chapter 19 — Return on Equity

## What the Book Claims
- D1 All Stocks: **12.29% CAGR** vs 11.22% universe
- "Modest edge, but base rates deteriorate over longer periods"
- "Not a reliable standalone factor"

## What We Found (2005–2026)

| | D1 (Highest ROE) | D10 (Lowest) | Spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| >$200M | +10.8% | +8.0% | **+2.8%** |
| >$5B | +11.9% | +10.6% | **+1.3%** |

Rolling 5yr positive: 82% (>$200M), 65% (>$5B)

## Verdict
**Modest and inconsistent, as the book predicted.**

ROE shows a +2.8% spread at >$200M — one of the few quality factors with any signal. But at >$5B it fades to +1.3% and only 65% of 5-year windows are positive.

The book called it "not reliable standalone" and our data confirms. ROE works better as part of a quality composite than alone.
