# Chapter 14 — Accruals-to-Price

## What the Book Claims
- Low accruals = high earnings quality → outperformance
- High accruals = potential earnings manipulation → underperformance

## What We Found (2005–2026)

| | D1 (Low Accruals) | D10 (High Accruals) | Spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| >$200M | +9.2% | +8.4% | **+0.9%** |
| >$5B | +10.7% | +9.5% | **+1.2%** |

Rolling 5yr positive: 65% (>$200M), 59% (>$5B)

## Verdict
**Marginal. The signal exists but is too weak to trade on.**

A +0.9 to +1.2% spread is barely above noise. Earnings quality matters in theory but doesn't generate enough alpha standalone to justify a strategy.
