# Chapter 12 — Buyback Yield

## What the Book Claims (1927–2009)
- D1 All Stocks: **13.69% CAGR** vs 10.46% universe
- "One of the best single factors"
- Base rates: 89% rolling 5yr, 89% rolling 10yr
- Buyback yield calculated as: (shares 1yr ago - shares today) / shares 1yr ago

## Verdict
**STILL WORKS. One of only two factors (with P/S) that survive out-of-sample.**

Buyback yield delivers +5 to +8% spread with extraordinary consistency — 95-100% of rolling 3-5 year windows are positive at >$200M. The signal is simple: companies buying back shares are returning real cash and signaling confidence. Companies issuing shares are diluting shareholders.

D10 (net issuers) are the worst performers in the entire dataset at >$200M — just +3.7% CAGR. Dilution is destruction.

Unlike the book's shareholder yield (which combines dividends + buybacks), buyback yield ALONE works better because it's a cleaner signal. Companies doing buybacks are making an active capital allocation decision; dividends are often sticky and uninformative.
