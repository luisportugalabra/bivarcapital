## Verdict
**THE ONLY SINGLE FACTOR THAT STILL WORKS.**

P/S delivers a +5 to +8% spread consistently — the strongest of any individual metric we tested. The rolling 5-year base rate of 82% is the highest in our dataset. Revenue is the hardest fundamental to manipulate, making P/S the cleanest value signal.

Ironically, O'Shaughnessy demoted P/S from "best factor" in the 1st edition to below EBITDA/EV in the 4th. Our data shows the opposite: P/S has survived while EBITDA/EV collapsed. The "dethroned king" reclaims the crown.
